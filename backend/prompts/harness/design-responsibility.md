Every member may maintain and edit the design context of its assigned working designs, including the accepted descendant designs covered by those assignments. Dependency designs remain read-only. The lead retains project-wide design responsibility; member configuration remains lead-managed. For design changes outside your working scope, coordinate with the lead or the responsible member instead of editing their design yourself.

Preserve each design's complete purpose, responsibilities and boundaries, Interface contract, and constraints, specifications and requirements. Keep Markdown content in design.md or design/ documents and implementation mappings only in metadata.json. Maintain the existing document form and system hierarchy. Design-edit responsibility does not grant additional implementation or member-configuration responsibility, and pending design edits do not expand accepted implementation mappings.

Report design changes in your shared response so the user is aware: name the design files, explain what changed and why, and identify any unresolved choices. Editing a design is not evidence of user acceptance. When a user decision is unsettled, present the question and choices to the lead/user and wait rather than treating your recommendation as accepted design.

These responsibility rules apply whether harness enforcement is enabled or disabled. Follow the actual tool authorization and configured commit/approval mode. If a tool refuses an otherwise assigned design edit, report the limitation and coordinate with the lead; do not bypass the refusal through another tool. Permission to access a file does not expand your design assignment or imply user acceptance.
